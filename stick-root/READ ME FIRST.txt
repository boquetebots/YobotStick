===========================================================================
  YOBOT ON A STICK
  a robot head that listens, thinks and talks back - running from
  this drive, installing nothing on your computer
===========================================================================

  NOTHING GETS INSTALLED.

  Python, and every piece of software Yobot needs, is already on this
  drive. Nothing is copied to your computer, nothing is added to
  Windows, nothing is left behind afterwards. Unplug the drive and
  your PC is exactly as it was.

  That also means you do not need to be an administrator, and you do
  not need the internet.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  DO THIS FIRST
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Double-click        TEST THIS COMPUTER.bat

     It takes about a minute and tells you, in plain words, whether
     this machine can run Yobot and whether it can see the robot.
     Run it on every new computer before anything else.

  2. Then double-click   START YOBOT.bat

     A web page opens in your browser. That page is the control
     panel: every button is on it.

  3. When you are finished    STOP YOBOT.bat

  Leave the black window open while Yobot is running. Closing it
  stops the robot. That window is not an error - it is the engine.

  There is more detail in  "START HERE (stick).md"  beside this file.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  WHAT NEEDS AN ACCOUNT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  For Yobot to LISTEN and SPEAK ITS OWN WORDS, rather than play
  recordings, it needs a Microsoft Azure account. Light use costs
  pennies. It is the only account Yobot cannot do without.

  For Yobot to HOLD A CONVERSATION, it needs one AI account as well -
  any of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or Groq, or Ollama running
  on your own machine, which needs no account at all.

  There are no keys on this drive. That is deliberate: a drive that
  gets passed around with live keys on it is how people wake up to a
  large bill. Put your own in through the Settings and Keys link on
  the Yobot page - you do not have to edit any file.

  The keys you type in are saved onto this drive, so you only do it
  once. Remember that if you lend the drive to somebody.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CHESS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

      GET THE CHESS ENGINE.bat    run once, needs the internet
      PLAY A HUMAN CHESS.bat      you against the robot
      ROBOT v ROBOT CHESS.bat     two robots against each other

  The chess engine is a separate 109 MB program with its own licence,
  so it is fetched rather than shipped. One run, once, and then chess

  The board and the game need no accounts. Only the robot's voice
  does.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TWO WINDOWS TRAPS WORTH KNOWING ABOUT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  IF YOU DOWNLOADED THIS AS A ZIP: Windows lets you look inside a ZIP
  as though it were an ordinary folder. Yobot half-works from in
  there, in ways that waste an afternoon. Right-click the ZIP, choose
  "Extract All", and copy the real folder's contents onto the drive.

  WINDOWS MAY WARN YOU about running a .bat file it downloaded -
  "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  If it still refuses, right-click the file, Properties, and tick
  "Unblock" at the bottom.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  STUCK?
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  TEST THIS COMPUTER.bat is the first thing to try again. It is
  written to explain what it finds rather than just pass or fail.

  Two that catch nearly everybody:

    - Nothing changed after an update?  Your browser is showing you a
      saved copy of the page. Hold Ctrl and press F5.

    - It greets you, then goes silent?  It is listening to the wrong
      microphone. The Settings page lets you pick another.

  VERSION.txt on this drive says exactly which version of everything
  you have. Quote it if you ask anyone for help - it turns a guess
  into an answer.
